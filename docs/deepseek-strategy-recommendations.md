## Tidbank for Fjärilspartiet: From Universal Time Banking to "Livstidskontot"

Your STR-001 already contains the seed: *"Individuell fulländning"* and *"Minimal nödvändig styrning"* imply a state that facilitates rather than controls. Tidbank fits perfectly, but let me adapt it to your framework.

**The concept, refined for Fjärilspartiet:**

**Livstidskontot (The Lifetime Account)** — a universal, individual account of state-supported time credits, accessible to every person from birth (with guardianship until the child can self-direct, perhaps from 12-13 in line with your youth co-actor principle).

**Core principles aligned with STR-001/101:**

1. **It's not just leave—it's autonomy infrastructure.** The account supports your *"individuell fulländning"* goal by giving people genuine temporal freedom to study, create, care, heal, or contribute civically without bureaucratic gatekeeping.

2. **It's age-integrated.** Children and youth have accounts too—perhaps initially smaller, accessed with guardian support, for approved developmental activities or civic participation. This tangibly demonstrates that the party takes young people's agency seriously.

3. **It's technologically transparent.** Built on the digital infrastructure you describe in OPS-100, with open-source management, privacy protection, and citizen oversight. No opaque algorithms deciding who "deserves" time.

4. **It replaces fragmented systems.** Parental leave, sick leave, study leave, care leave—folded into one dignified, flexible account. This exemplifies *"adaptiv styrning"* and reduces bureaucratic complexity.

5. **It's funded progressively but universally.** A flat, broad-based contribution model that ensures everyone has skin in the game, while the wealthy contribute more in absolute terms through the tax system.

**Why this matters for a Spiral Dynamics Yellow party:** Tidbank isn't a policy tweak—it's a structural intervention that shifts the system's attractor from "you work to prove your worth" toward "you have inherent worth, and time is a resource for your flourishing."

## Electoral Strategy for Fjärilspartiet: The Butterfly Method

Your party faces a unique challenge: you're operating from Yellow/Turquoise consciousness in a political landscape dominated by Orange achievement, Green pluralism, and Blue traditionalism. A conventional electoral strategy would fail—you'd be incomprehensible or threatening to most voters.

**What I'd propose instead:**

### Phase 1: The Chrysalis (Building Invisible Legitimacy)

Don't run for office yet. Instead, create undeniable demonstrations of your principles in action.

- **Identify 2-3 small to medium-sized municipalities** (5,000–30,000 people) facing complex, cross-cutting problems that defy conventional solutions: rural decline, youth outmigration, integration challenges, local food system fragility.
- **Approach them not as a party, but as a civic innovation partner.** Offer to facilitate a community-driven process using your Butterfly Framework: citizen assemblies with genuine youth participation, systems mapping of local challenges, evidence-based policy co-creation.
- **Document everything openly.** Publish methodologies, failures, and successes. Build a reputation for being genuinely useful, not ideologically driven.
- **Grow your membership through demonstrated competence, not charismatic promises.** The people who join because they've *experienced* this way of working will be deeply committed.

### Phase 2: The Emergence (First Electoral Tests)

- **Target a single municipality where you've built trust.** Run not as "the party with all the answers" but as "the party that has demonstrated a better way of asking questions together."
- **Your candidates should be people who participated in the Phase 1 processes**—authentic community leaders, not career politicians.
- **The campaign promise:** We will govern this municipality with the same transparent, adaptive, age-inclusive methods we've already been practicing here. We will turn this place into a living laboratory of democratic innovation.
- **Win or lose, treat the campaign as another demonstration project.** Publish a post-campaign analysis: what worked, what didn't, what we learned about communicating Yellow-stage ideas to a mixed-stage public.

### Phase 3: The Metamorphosis (Proving the Model)

- **If you win:** Govern exactly as promised. Radically transparent budgeting. Youth co-decision-making on relevant issues. Continuous citizen feedback loops. Treat every policy as a hypothesis to be tested. Document it all.
- **Let success be undeniable.** If your municipality measurably improves on well-being indicators, if young people start returning instead of leaving, if trust in local government rises—this becomes a story that transcends partisan media narratives.
- **Then, and only then, scale.** First to neighboring municipalities who've been watching, then regionally, then nationally.

### Why This Approach Fits Fjärilspartiet

- It embodies *"minimal nödvändig styrning"*—you're not seeking power for its own sake.
- It's *evidensbaserad*—you earn the right to be heard through demonstrated results.
- It respects *"adaptiv styrning"*—the strategy evolves with learning.
- It sidesteps the trap of trying to explain Yellow consciousness to an Orange/Green audience; you let them *experience* the difference.
- It builds a movement of *practitioners*, not just voters.
