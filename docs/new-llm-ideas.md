## Electoral Strategy 
Target the "exhausted majority"—the 70% of Swedes tired of polarization. Instead of launching nationally, we'd aim to take over one or two medium-sized, struggling municipalities first. Run them as radical transparency, evidence-based governance testbeds. Make them the best places to live and do business in Sweden. Let success be the campaign for national relevance.

## A Modernized Social Contract: The "Tidbank" (Time Bank)

The welfare state was built for a world of male breadwinners and 40-year careers. This party recognizes a world of gig work, serial careers, and an aging population.

- **The "Tidbank" System:** Every citizen gets a lifetime "time bank" of state-funded leave, beyond the current parental and sick leave. You can draw from it for:
    - Lifelong learning or a mid-career sabbatical.
    - Intensive family care (elderly parents, a sick partner).
    - Your own mental health recovery.
    - A "civic service" year for community work.
    This is funded by a flat, broad-based tax, removing the stigma and bureaucracy of separate insurance systems. It’s autonomy insurance for the 21st century.

## Governance Model: Modular & Transparent

Rather than a fixed party line on everything, the party would operate like a **platform**:

* Policy areas are divided into “modules” (energy, housing, migration, defense, etc.)
* Each module is:

  * transparently developed
  * continuously updated
  * open to expert and citizen input

Think:

* version-controlled politics
* public drafts, iteration, feedback loops

Almost like a blend of:

* Wikipedia (open knowledge)
* GitHub (version control)
* democratic accountability
